Take control of your household money

Features

Everything you need to run your home budget

A simple, focused tool that keeps your household money clear and under control.

🏠

Home Dashboard

See the month's remainder, available money, debts and goals at a glance, plus a live activity feed.

🗓️

Monthly Budget

Record your monthly income and track the remainder for every month.

📊

Reports & Charts

Bar, pie and line charts for spending by category and income vs. expenses trends.

🏷️

Price Tracking

Save prices of goods (sugar, fuel, …) with a unit, so you always know today's cost.

🛒

Groceries

Quick entry or itemized mode with quantities, units, prices and an automatic total.

🧾

Bills

Gas, water, electricity, fuel, internet, rent and custom categories.

🤝

Debts

Track money lent to others and money you owe, with partial payments and progress.

📦

Other Expenses

Log any uncategorized expense in seconds.

🐷

Savings

An automatic savings balance derived from income, expenses, debts and goals.

🎯

Goals

Create savings goals with targets, fund them from your balance and watch progress.

🛡️

Backup & Restore

Export all your data to a JSON file and import it back any time.

🌙

Dark Mode & RTL

A polished Arabic-first interface with RTL layout and a light/dark theme.

Modules

Pages & navigation

Organized into three groups for quick access from the side menu.

Home  ──┬── GROUP A : Overview
        │     ├── Home       ── dashboard & activity
        │     ├── Monthly    ── monthly income
        │     ├── Reports    ── charts & analytics
        │     └── Prices     ── price tracking
        │
        ├── GROUP B : Spending
        │     ├── Groceries  ── quick / detailed
        │     ├── Bills      ── utilities & rent
        │     ├── Debts      ── for us / on us
        │     └── Other      ── misc expenses
        │
        └── GROUP C : Growth
              ├── Savings    ── computed balance
              └── Goals      ── savings targets
Tech Stack

Lightweight and dependency-free

Built with plain web technologies and packaged for Android.

🟨 Vanilla JavaScript 🎨 CSS3 (custom themes) 📱 Capacitor 6 🤖 Android 💾 localStorage 📈 Canvas charts 🌐 RTL support
Privacy & Architecture

Private by design

No servers, no accounts, no analytics — your data never leaves your device.

🔒

100% Local

All data is stored in your browser/device storage — nothing is uploaded.

📴

Offline-First

Works without any internet connection, anywhere.

🛡️

Budget Guard

Prevents recording spending that exceeds your available money.
